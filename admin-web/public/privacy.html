Privacy Policy

Housie Tambola King · Effective 19 November 2026

Who we are

Housie Tambola King ("the app", "we") is a casual online Housie / Tambola game for India, published by Project Bluri Media. Contact: projectblurimedia@gmail.com.

No real money. Ticket prices and prize amounts in the app are casual numbers for fun between friends. The app has no wallet, no payments and no real-money gaming.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your name and mobile numberTo create your account, log you in and show your name to other players in your rooms.
Your 4-digit PINTo log you in. It is stored only as a one-way encrypted hash — nobody, including us, can read it.
Avatar, language and app settingsTo show your profile and the app in your language.
Game activity: rooms, tickets, claims, wins and the casual amountsTo run games, decide winners and show room standings.
Room chat messagesTo show them to the other players in that room.
Push notification tokenTo notify you about your rooms (for example, a join request or a game starting).
Crash reports: error message, app screen, app version, phone OS versionTo find and fix crashes. They do not include your messages or tickets.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or payment details, and we do not show ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other players in the same room see your name, avatar, the tickets you take, your claims, wins and room chat. Your mobile number is never shown to other players.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database and accounts, servers in Mumbai, India), Expo (app updates and push notifications) and Google (Play Store, Android notifications). They process data only to provide their service.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if Indian law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account, rooms and game history: while your account exists.
  • Crash reports: 30 days. Claim logs used to stop spam: 2 days.
  • When you delete your account, your profile and personal data are deleted. Past game results may stay in other players' room standings without your name.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. Or email us and we will do it within 7 days.
  • Change your name, avatar and language in Profile.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it: projectblurimedia@gmail.com.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Game data is protected by database access rules so players can only read what they are allowed to; PINs are hashed; after 5 wrong PINs an account is locked for 15 minutes.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
