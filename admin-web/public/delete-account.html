Delete your account

Housie Tambola King · Project Bluri Media

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Housie Tambola King and log in with your mobile number and PIN.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are logged out.

Can't open the app? Ask us by email

Email projectblurimedia@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number you registered with. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, PIN, avatar and settings.
  • Your login and push-notification token.
  • Your room memberships, tickets, claims, wins and room chat messages.

What may be kept

  • Rooms you hosted stay for the other players in them, without you as host.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 30 days without your name, then deleted automatically.
